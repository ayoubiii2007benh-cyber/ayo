Cookie Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026 · Version 2026-09-30

The short version: one essential cookie keeps you signed in. Google Analytics cookies are set only if you accept. Nothing is used for advertising.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Websites also use similar technologies such as your browser’s local storage and IndexedDB. This policy covers all of them, and explains how they relate to our Privacy Policy.

2. What we use

Until you press Accept (or turn on Analytics in Manage), the Google Analytics script is not downloaded, no _ga cookies exist and no data goes to Google. We use Google Consent Mode v2 with analytics and all advertising signals set to “denied” by default, and we keep Google signals and ad personalisation switched off. The data helps us see how many people visit and which parts of the site are used, and is not used to build advertising profiles. Google acts as our processor for this and is also described in its own partner-sites information.

Content from other websites that you choose to load

  • YouTube — only if you pick a YouTube video as a background or for music. The video is shown through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, but YouTube/Google may still use cookies or similar storage and receive your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Images, videos or audio from a web link you paste — loaded by your browser straight from that website, which may set its own cookies.
  • Google Gemini — only if you add your own API key for the AI assistant; requests go straight from your browser to Google.
  • Sign in with Google / Microsoft / Facebook — only if you click those buttons; those companies have their own cookies on their own pages.

We do not use advertising or social-media tracking cookies, and we do not load fonts from Google (the font is hosted on our own site).

3. How to change your choice

  • Click Cookie settings in the footer of any page (or the button above) to accept or reject analytics at any time. Turning analytics off also deletes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.
  • You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the essential cookie means you cannot stay signed in.
  • Google provides a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics everywhere.

We will ask again after 12 months or if what we use changes materially.
