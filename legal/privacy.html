1. Who we are

Pomodoro Focus (pomodorofocus.site) is a Pomodoro timer, daily planner and social study app (the “Study Lounge”). It is run by the site’s owner, who is the “controller” of your personal data under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR. In this policy “we”, “us” and “our” mean the operator of pomodorofocus.site.

For anything about your data, write to help.pomodorofocus@gmail.com. That address handles data access, deletion and every other privacy request.

2. What we collect and why

If you only use the app as a guest

Your timer settings, tasks, statistics, XP and missions, theme, background and music choices, AI-assistant chat history, and any profile photo you pick are saved on your own device (browser storage and IndexedDB). They are not sent to our servers. Clearing your browser data removes them. Guests still make a few small technical requests to our server (for example to show the number of people online), and our hosting providers see your IP address as part of delivering the site (see “Technical data” below).

Voice, camera and screen sharing in rooms

These work directly between the people in a room (peer-to-peer, WebRTC). Our server only passes along the connection handshake. We do not record, store or listen to audio, video or screen content. To connect, your browser contacts public STUN servers run by Google (stun.l.google.com), which see your IP address, and the other people in the room can technically see it too as part of a direct connection. Your browser asks for microphone, camera or screen access only when you turn those features on, and you can stop at any time.

Technical data

  • IP address and request details. Every website receives these. Our hosting and network providers (see section 5) process them to deliver the site and to block attacks, and keep their own short-lived logs. Our application does not store IP addresses in its database and does not write them to its own logs. To limit abuse, it holds them briefly in memory (for up to about an hour) to count requests, then forgets them.
  • Login attempt counters. To stop password guessing we count failed logins per username in memory for up to an hour. This is not stored in the database.
  • Precise location. We do not collect it. We do not use GPS or ask for your location.
  • Cookies and similar storage: see section 4 and our Cookie Policy.

Things you choose to do yourself

  • AI assistant. If you paste your own Google Gemini API key into Settings, your key is saved only in your browser, and what you type to the assistant is sent directly from your browser to Google. It does not pass through our server.
  • YouTube or media backgrounds/music. If you choose a YouTube video as a background or play one, your browser loads content from YouTube (Google). If you paste a link to an image, video or audio file, your browser loads it from that website. Those sites can see your IP address and may set their own cookies.

3. Our legal bases (GDPR Article 6)

  • Contract — to provide the account and social features you ask for (sign-in, friends, messages, rooms, leaderboard, password reset).
  • Consent — for Google Analytics and any other non-essential cookies. You can say no, and you can change your mind at any time via “Cookie settings” in the footer. Withdrawing consent does not affect what happened before.
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure and free of abuse (rate limits, brute-force protection, CAPTCHA), fixing problems, and keeping a record of agreement to our terms. We weigh these against your rights and keep them proportionate.
  • Legal obligation — where the law requires us to keep or hand over information.

Giving us an email address is optional, but without one we cannot email a password-reset code.

4. Cookies and analytics

We use one essential first-party cookie to keep you signed in (only after you log in). With your permission only, we use Google Analytics (measurement ID G-Y0B0W7YJ78) to count visits and understand how the site is used. Before you choose, Google Analytics is not loaded at all and sets no cookies; we use Google Consent Mode v2 with every signal set to “denied” by default. We switch off Google signals and ad personalisation. If you reject, nothing from Google Analytics runs.

Full details, names and durations are in the Cookie Policy. You can change your choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page.

We do not show adverts and we do not sell or rent personal data.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access your data and get a copy — Profile → Privacy & data → Download my data gives you a machine-readable JSON file straight away.
  • Correct it — edit your profile, or email us for anything you cannot edit.
  • Erase it — Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my account and data permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it. This cannot be undone.
  • Restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Data portability — the download above is portable.
  • Withdraw consent — use “Cookie settings” in the footer for analytics; closing your account ends everything else.
  • Not be subject to automated decisions with legal effect. We do not make any.

Email help.pomodorofocus@gmail.com for any of these, including if you cannot sign in. We may ask you to prove the account is yours. We aim to answer within one month. Exercising your rights is free.

9. How we protect data

  • The whole site is served over HTTPS and tells browsers to insist on it (HSTS).
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt; password-reset codes are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Your login is an HttpOnly, Secure cookie that scripts on the page cannot read, protected against cross-site request forgery.
  • Sign-up, login and password reset are rate-limited, use a bot check, and slow down repeated wrong guesses.
  • A strict Content-Security-Policy and careful handling of user-generated text protect against script injection.
  • Access to other people’s data is checked on the server for every request.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the regulator where the law requires it.

10. Children

Pomodoro Focus is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you live somewhere that sets a higher minimum age for agreeing to online services (for example 16 in some EU countries), you must be at least that age, or have a parent or guardian’s permission where the law allows that. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.
